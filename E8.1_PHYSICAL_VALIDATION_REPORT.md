## 6. Deterministic Plan Validation & Invariant Enforcement

PlanValidator enforces all critical physical invariants before any hardware packet is dispatched:

1. **Optical Safety Invariant**: Prevents cold power-on loops if projector is unresponsive.
2. **HDMI Single-Port Invariant**: Rejects `PROJECTOR_SWITCH_HDMI2` and `PROJECTOR_SWITCH_HDMI3` deterministically (`ErrorCode.UNSUPPORTED_CAPABILITY`).
3. **Climate Safety Invariants**:
   - Enforces valid temperature range $[16^\circ\text{C}, 30^\circ\text{C}]$ (`ErrorCode.OUT_OF_RANGE`).
   - Rejects `HEAT` mode on cooling-only Inverter AC (`ErrorCode.INVALID_ENUM`).
4. **Zero-Mutation on Rejection**: When validation fails, `PlanExecutor` immediately halts with `OverallExecutionStatus.FAILED` and issues 0 hardware commands.
